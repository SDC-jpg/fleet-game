# Leviathan attacks (plan, agreed 2026-11-19)

The user's spec, with their answers to the open questions. Numbers are starting points for tuning.

## Phase 1: free swimming, body hits, health, hurts everyone
- Nothing moves it: no ship pushing, Repulse, Gravity Well, Scramble or Force Wall blocking. It always swims its own path.
- **Freeze slows it** (does not stop it).
- Every body piece hits: a ship touching any piece takes damage (share of its max health, like the beam) and is knocked away; a short cooldown per ship between hits.
- Health × 5.
- It hurts enemy ships too (body hits, beam, balls, blood). **One-way:** enemy ships ignore it and keep attacking you.

## Phase 2: ram (upgrade of its lunge)
Warning (slows, head pulls back, flash, roar) → fast straight charge at where your fleet was → wide recovery turn. Head does extra damage and knockback while ramming. Never during the beam.

## Phase 3: energy balls
Every 6 s of combat (your flagship in range) a crackling ball (about the charge ball's size) spawns from its body and drifts toward your fleet with some wander, slower than your ships. Every 2 s it pulses (tell, then ring) damaging every ship in a small area. Max 10; invulnerable; **your shots pass through them**; they vanish when the Leviathan dies.

## Phase 4: wounds (every 1/20 of its health)
From the point of that hit: a burst of glowing purple blood shots in all directions (damage) and 15–20 parasites. Hits on the head or tail give blood but no parasites. Hit flash: **a small flash on every hit**, and at each wound **a big flash rippling weaker into the neighbouring pieces**.

## Phase 5: parasites
About half a T1 ship; weak, can be shot like normal enemies. They latch onto your ships; each latched one slows your whole fleet (capped). Dash shakes them loose. **At most 80 alive** at once.

All new Leviathan effects are **purple** placeholders (flat style), listed in ShipArt Effects.
