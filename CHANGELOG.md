# What's new

Player-facing changes, newest first. When a new version goes live, the lines
added here since the last version are posted to the Discord build-log channel.
Keep each change on one line starting with `- `.

## 2026-11-11

- The Lancer's Lance is now much easier to see: a big white-hot bolt with a muzzle flash and a glowing trail.
- The body shop window keeps a fixed size instead of resizing while you shop.
- The ship you fly is easier to spot: a white arrow bobs above it, and its ring is thicker and brighter.
- A ship's main class icon is now much bigger than its second class icon.
- **The body shop is open!** Every few waves a station visits (follow the green arrow). Fly in to spend scrap: swap modules between ships, convert modules to another class, make modules rare, pick your combo, and repair. The game pauses while you shop.
- **Loot!** Rare modules make abilities noticeably stronger: longer Lances, longer-lasting shields, twice the missiles. Rare wrecks have a pink marker, and the HUD shows how many rare modules your ship carries.
- **Elite enemies** appear from wave 5: they glow pink, are much tougher, and always leave a rare wreck.
- Big enemies now break apart into small wrecks of their classes when destroyed (sometimes they still leave one big wreck).
- You now earn **scrap** from kills (shown in the Run panel). It will buy things at the upcoming body shop.
- New art for tier 2 ships.
- New HUD at the bottom of the screen: your run stats, plus a panel showing the ship you fly, its classes, health and ability cooldowns. In a pixel font!
- Class icons above ships now go dark while their ability recharges, and fill back up from the bottom.
- Bigger ships have more boost: +50% boost time per tier.
- Your first kill always leaves a wreck, so you get a wingman early.
- With small ships, the mouse wheel now switches between classes (the healthiest Lancer, Knight or Ballista).
- **Ship classes!** Every small ship is now a Lancer (fires a Lance that pierces a whole line of ships), a Knight (raises Aegis shields on itself and nearby allies) or a Ballista (launches homing missiles). A rank icon above each ship and wreck shows its class, so you can choose what to pick up.
- When ships merge, their classes add up: 8 Lancers make a big, powerful Lancer, while a mix makes a ship with several weaker abilities. Like merges with like.
- Combos: ships with two strong classes get a bonus. Mirror Guard (shields reflect shots), Splinter Volley (missiles burst into lances), Retaliation (shields fire missiles back).
- New controls: **right-click** uses your ship's main ability, **Space** dashes, and the **mouse wheel** switches between your biggest ships to pick your ability. Left-click fires.
- 8 ships now merge into the next size (it was 4 for testing), starting at fleet value 16.
- The boost ring around your ship is now a smooth line instead of dots.
- Enemies have classes too: watch out for their shields and missiles.
- Bigger ships turn more slowly (25% slower per tier), so they feel heavier and you have to aim ahead. Small ships stay nimble.
- Boost (Shift) now runs on stamina: about 3 seconds from full, shown by the ring around your ship, which refills when you let go. Boost is faster to make up for it.
- The dash is much stronger: it now starts at 8× speed and carries your fleet much further.
- Testing setup: bigger ships come 4× faster (4 ships merge into the next tier, starting at fleet value 8), and big enemies leave wrecks more often. Fleet values are smaller numbers for now.
- New: a **tuning settings page** listing every adjustable number in the game with its current value. It updates itself with each new version.
- Dash cooldown shortened from 30 to 18 seconds.
- The dash now goes further when the camera is zoomed out, so it stays useful with a big fleet.
- New: **right-click to dash**. Your whole fleet bursts to 3× speed for a moment, with a speed trail. 30 second cooldown.
- Your ship is 20% faster flying toward your cursor and about 18% slower backing away.
- **Esc pauses** the game. It also pauses by itself when you click another window.
- End screen report: ships destroyed and lost, and a graph of your fleet's value over the run.
- Waves come slightly faster as the game goes on, with a random rhythm: some arrive early, some late.
- Merging 16 ships now fires a ring of shots in every direction.
- Bigger ships fire a little slower but hit harder (same damage overall).
- Wrecks have a yellow marker so they're easy to spot when zoomed out.
- Bigger wrecks make a brighter sound when you recruit them.
- Fixed: the survival timer kept running after your fleet was destroyed.
- Wingmen now fire where you aim: they only turn up to 15° away from their leader's facing. Enemy fleets follow the same rule.
- Sound effects! Press **M** or click the speaker button to turn them on or off.
- Big fleets (tier 3 and up) are less zoomed out, so ships stay readable.
- Frames-per-second counter in the HUD.
- Ship tiers: 16 ships of one tier merge into one bigger, stronger ship.
